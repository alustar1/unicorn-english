Privacy Policy

Unicorn English · Last updated: [DATE]

Unicorn English is an English learning app for children, made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). We built it to collect as little as possible. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

What stays on the device

  • Learning progress: levels, stars, streaks, practice calendar and review lists are saved on the device. So a child keeps their progress on the family's other devices or a new phone, it is also kept in your own iCloud account (iPhone and iPad) or your own Google account backup (Android). We cannot see or access this copy.
  • Voice: in the Say It! game the microphone is used to check the spoken sentence. Speech recognition runs on the device. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Reminders: practice reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from the device. The copy in your iCloud or Google backup is managed in your iCloud or Google account settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see payment details. To check whether a subscription is active we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Apple Kids Category rules and COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no accounts. The paywall, Parent Corner and external links are behind a parent check.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you or your child. If you have a question, or want us to confirm anything under GDPR (EU/UK) or KVKK (Türkiye), email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
