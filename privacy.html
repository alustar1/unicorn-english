Privacy Policy

Unicorn English · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Unicorn English is an English learning app for children, made by Banu Eryılmaz Kuzu (App Store) and Muhammet Kuzu (Google Play) ("we"). We built it to collect as little as possible. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

What stays on the device

  • Learning progress: levels, stars, streaks, practice calendar and review lists are saved on the device. So a child keeps their progress on the family's other devices or a new phone, it is also kept in your own iCloud account (iPhone and iPad) or your own Google account backup (Android). We cannot see or access this copy.
  • Voice: the microphone is used when your child speaks in the app. Say It!, the word levels and the stories check the spoken words with speech recognition that runs on the device; Chant Time only measures how loud the child is, to show that it hears them. Parrot Talk plays your child's own voice back: those few seconds of sound stay in the app's temporary storage only until the parrot has played them, then they are deleted. No voice is kept or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Reminders: practice reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
  • Child profiles: a parent can add up to four children and give each a first name or nickname. The name is optional, is shown only on the home screen, and stays on the device. It is never sent to us or anyone else and is not part of the iCloud copy. Each child's stars, stickers and traced letters are stored the same way as other progress.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from the device. The copy in your iCloud or Google backup is managed in your iCloud or Google account settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see payment details. To check whether a subscription is active we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchase receipt and, as with any internet request, the device's IP address. It is used only to confirm purchases and is not used for advertising or tracking. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Apple Kids Category rules and COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not use any advertising identifier, analytics or tracking. There are no accounts, no chat and no social features. We do not share information about children with anyone for advertising or marketing. The paywall, Parent Corner and external links are behind a parent check. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you or your child. A parent can review or delete everything the app stores at any time: reset progress in Parent Corner, or delete the app. If you have a question, or want us to confirm anything under GDPR (EU/UK) or KVKK (Türkiye), email lizenapps@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
